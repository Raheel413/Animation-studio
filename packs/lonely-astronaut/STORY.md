# Lonely Astronaut: story engine

Why Episode 1 felt like a film and not a faceless video. Apply all ten to every episode.

1. **A person who wants something.** Story over information: one want, one obstacle, one choice.
2. **Hook = a question on screen, no words.** "FINAL VERIFIED SIGNAL ... ADDRESSED TO: ASTRO" + his hand pulling back from PLAY. Answer it no sooner than ~60 s later.
3. **Plant early, pay off three times.** One small concrete motif (height marks, a sound, an object) introduced in the first minute, echoed in the middle, paid off at the climax and the last shot.
4. **The choice must cost something, and be made before the reward is known.** Astro erases 4,012 messages to save a stranger, then learns it is his daughter.
5. **The twist answers the title.** The last message Earth sent was a person, and it was meant for him.
6. **Restraint.** About 170 spoken words in 5.5 minutes. Silence before every big moment.
7. **Audience learns it with Astro.** Hide the answer physically (frost, static, a covered label) and reveal it on screen.
8. **Colour carries meaning.** Cyan memory dies, amber life switches on.
9. **Film grammar.** Letterbox, tiny-figure wides, eye close-ups at the peaks, slow pushes, inserts of hands.
10. **One world.** Same Astro, same ship sets, recurring props, so viewers come back for him.

## Structure template (~5:00)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00-0:20 | Wordless hook: a screen, an alarm or an object poses the question; Astro's eyes react; he hesitates |
| 0:20-0:30 | Title card |
| 0:30-1:05 | His routine and the person he misses; plant the motif |
| 1:05-2:10 | The inciting message or event (the main spoken section); intercut 2-3 inserts so it isn't a talking head |
| 2:10-2:40 | Decision to act; one short Astro line |
| 2:40-3:20 | Journey, **with its own danger or question** (Ep 1's weakest stretch) |
| 3:20-4:00 | Arrival; the cost is laid out on a screen; the choice; the erasure or loss shown through the motif |
| 4:00-4:45 | Reveal, hidden until the last second; 2-3 lines of dialogue max |
| 4:45-5:30 | Motif paid off a final time, the ship turns home, reframing end card |
